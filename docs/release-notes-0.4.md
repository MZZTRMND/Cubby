## Cubby 0.4

**New**
- **Customize window**: Settings → Look → Customize… opens a live preview where you set the folder's theme and size.
- **Themes**: 8 color themes. Each has a light and a dark version and switches with your system appearance. They replace the free color pickers.
- **More items in the folder**: the folder can now show up to 10 items.

**Changed**
- **Drag summon**: when you drag something in another app, the folder now pops up next to the pointer instead of in the middle of the screen.

**Install**
Download `Cubby-0.4.zip`, unzip it and move Cubby.app to Applications. Cubby isn't notarized, so the first time you open it, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
